# AI Playground – CodeflowX  
## Training, Evaluation, and Experimentation of Multimodal Prompts

### What is AI Playground?

AI Playground is CodeflowX’s advanced environment for experimentation, training, and validation of generative AI prompts and models, designed for product, innovation, data science, and enterprise development teams. It enables testing, comparing, and versioning prompts across multiple models, with true multimodal support (text, images, documents, audio), facilitating human supervision and continuous improvement of conversational and output quality.

---

## Key Features

- **Multimodal Chat**:  
  Chat interface that allows sending text, images, documents, audio, and video, simulating real-world use cases for enterprise assistants and copilots.

- **Prompt Versioning**:  
  Professional semantic versioning system (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH) for prompts, with history, restoration, and automatic version suggestions following engineering best practices.

- **Template Management**:  
  Save, name, and reuse prompts as templates, with usage, performance, and evolution metrics per version.

- **Integrated Analytics**:  
  Real-time metrics panel: number of messages, tokens consumed, attachments, average response time, and usage per prompt version.

- **RAG and Smart Routing Testing**:  
  Dedicated playgrounds for testing Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) and smart model routing rules, with fallback simulation and cost tracking.

- **Simultaneous Translation**:  
  Translation playground in 25+ major international languages, with automatic language detection, batch document processing, and audio input.

- **Minimalist, Professional UI**:  
  Clean, distraction-free interface with sidebar navigation, resizable panels, and UX designed for professional teams.

- **Session Import/Export**:  
  Save and share chat sessions and configurations for reproducibility and collaboration.

## What Problems Does AI Playground Solve?

- **Prompt training and fine-tuning** without long development cycles or deployments.
- **Model validation and comparison** in real scenarios, accelerating iteration and quality improvement.

- **Risk reduction** by enabling exhaustive testing before production.
- **Empowerment of non-technical teams** to experiment and validate ideas without code.
