## The easiest and safest way to experiment with generative AI

### What is AI Playground?

AI Playground is a simple, visual tool that lets anyone test, compare, and improve AI assistants—no programming required. You can chat with the AI, upload images, documents, or audio, and see how it responds in real time.

---

## What can you do with AI Playground?

- **Chat with AI**: Type messages and get smart answers, just like talking to an expert.
- **Upload files**: Attach images, documents, or audio and let the AI analyze and help you.
- **Easy translation**: Translate texts and documents into 25+ languages in seconds.
- **Try different versions**: Save your tests and revisit them anytime, seeing how things have improved.
- **See results and stats**: Discover how many messages you’ve sent, how fast the AI responds, and how it improves over time.
- **Compare and choose the best**: Test different models and pick the one that fits your needs.
